<b>Q: How to pronounce the metal band -- MØL?</b>

**A:** MØL (muhl)

The Danish blackgaze band **MØL** pronounces its name approximately like **"muhl"**, with a rounded vowel produced by the Danish letter **Ø**.

For an English speaker:

* The closest approximation is **"muhl"** (rhyming loosely with "pull," but with lips rounded more tightly).
* It is **not** pronounced "mole" or "mall."

The letter **Ø** is a distinct Danish and Norwegian vowel, roughly intermediate between the vowels in English *bird* and French *bleu*. English has no exact equivalent.

The band's choice of **MØL** is also meaningful: in Danish, **"møl"** means **"moth,"** which fits the atmospheric and melancholic themes of their music.
