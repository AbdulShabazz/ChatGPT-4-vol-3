### Acting categories

* **Matt Damon — Best Actor:** Serious nomination buzz, with some writers calling it a career-level performance. AwardsWatch currently places him inside its predicted five, while other projections describe him as near the nomination cutoff or even a potential winner. This is credible buzz, but not yet a settled consensus. ([AwardsWatch][1])
* **Anne Hathaway — Supporting Actress:** Currently the clearest acting possibility after Damon. Multiple prognosticators identify Penelope as the film’s standout supporting role. ([AwardsWatch][1])
* **Robert Pattinson — Supporting Actor:** Considered the strongest male supporting possibility, although limited screen time and competition within the ensemble could prevent a nomination. ([AwardsWatch][1])
* **Samantha Morton — Supporting Actress:** Receiving conspicuous praise for Circe, but reportedly appears primarily in one concentrated sequence. That makes her a potential critics’ favorite whose campaign may be displaced by Hathaway. ([All My Life... I Wanted To Be A Blogster][2])
* **John Leguizamo:** A fringe supporting-actor possibility based on strong notices, but likely dependent on Universal mounting a deliberate campaign. ([All My Life... I Wanted To Be A Blogster][2])
* **Tom Holland, Lupita Nyong’o and Zendaya:** Mentioned online, but their relatively limited roles currently make them less convincing Oscar candidates. ([AwardsWatch][1])

**Peripheral possibilities:** Makeup and Hairstyling, plus the closing Travis Scott–James Blake song in Original Song, have been mentioned, but both presently appear less secure than the primary craft categories. ([All My Life... I Wanted To Be A Blogster][2])

The realistic early projection is therefore **approximately 10–13 nominations**, with an upper-end scenario reaching 14 or 15 if Damon, Hathaway, Pattinson and the secondary crafts all survive the season. The most probable wins presently appear to be **Cinematography, Sound, Visual Effects, Production Design and possibly Casting**, while Best Picture and Director depend heavily on the unreleased autumn competition.
