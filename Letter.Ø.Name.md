**Q: What is the name of this letter: ø?**

**A:** **ø** is called **Latin small letter O with stroke**, commonly **O-slash** or **slashed O**. It is a separate letter in Danish, Norwegian, and Faroese.

**Q: Any special pronunciation?**

**A:** Yes. **Ø is not pronounced like ordinary “o.”** In Danish and Norwegian, it is typically a rounded front vowel, similar to:

* **French** *eu* in *deux*
* **German** *ö* in *schön*
* Rough English approximation: the vowel in **“bird”**, but with the lips rounded

Its exact sound varies with the word, dialect, vowel length, and language.
